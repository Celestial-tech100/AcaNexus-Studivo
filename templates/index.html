About

Why We Built
AcaNexus

AcaNexus : Studivo was born out of a frustration shared by thousands of hostel-dwelling engineering students — juggling WhatsApp groups for notes, separate apps for tasks, and a noticeboard for mess menus.

We set out to build the single platform that understands the dual identity of a student: a learner striving for academic excellence and a resident navigating the everyday logistics of hostel life.
